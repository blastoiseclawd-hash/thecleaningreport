# TheCleaningReport Desk Image Prompts

These prompts are for transparent editorial desk visuals only. Do not generate human portraits or imply that any desk is a real individual reviewer.

## Global Rules

- Use abstract marks, tool silhouettes, or editorial symbols.
- No faces, people, mascots, fictional reviewers, or lifestyle scenes.
- Keep the style flat, crisp, and readable at small sizes.
- The system should feel practical, durable, and indoor-cleaning focused.
- Prefer the existing site palette:
  - deep espresso `#23150f`
  - charcoal navy `#182028`
  - sage `#8ea087`
  - forest `#4F7B62`
  - warm sand `#f4eadf`
  - off-white `#fffdf9`
- Icons should communicate desk function, not product branding.

## Everyday Cleaning Desk

Prompt:
"Design an abstract editorial desk icon for a practical indoor home-cleaning brand. The symbol should communicate simple defaults, daily floor care, and low-friction upkeep. Use tidy stacked forms, a compact vacuum silhouette, or organized horizontal sweep lines. No people, no faces, no mascots. Flat vector style, charcoal navy, warm sand, sage, and forest accents. Must feel calm, useful, and easy to scan at small size."

## Floor Care and Pet Mess Desk

Prompt:
"Design an abstract editorial desk icon for a practical indoor home-cleaning brand. The symbol should communicate robot vacuums, pet hair cleanup, and higher-friction floor mess. Use circular robot geometry, layered floor-path lines, or a simplified brush-roll motif that suggests tangles being cleared. No people, no faces, no mascots. Flat vector style, deep espresso, charcoal navy, forest, sage, and warm sand. Must feel pressure-tested, comparative, and household-tough."

## Deep Clean and Maintenance Desk

Prompt:
"Design an abstract editorial desk icon for a practical indoor home-cleaning brand. The symbol should communicate steam mops, specialty-cleaning tools, and surface-safe deep-clean decisions. Use a simplified mop head, geometric steam lines, or a tool-and-grid composition that suggests floor compatibility and caution. No people, no faces, no mascots. Flat vector style, charcoal navy, sage, warm sand, and subtle forest accents. Must feel precise, surface-aware, and evidence-led."
