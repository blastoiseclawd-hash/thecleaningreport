## Scope guard

The page teaches sink cleaning technique. It does NOT:
- Rank or review cleaner products (that is the hub's job)
- Cover appliance cleaning broadly (cooktop, fridge, dishwasher — separate technique pages if they merit)
- Cover cookware descaling (Bar Keepers Friend has a specific cookware use case; linked out, not retaught)
- Cover non-stainless sinks (porcelain, composite, copper — separate pages if they merit)

## Why this page is additive (not overlap)

The `/best-stainless-steel-cleaner` hub has a short "which cleaner for which use" section but does NOT teach the technique in depth. Readers searching "how to clean stainless steel sink" are asking a method question, not a product question. Sending them to the money page frustrates method-seeking intent. This support page catches them, teaches the technique with the desk's Surface Protector voice, and routes buyers who want a product recommendation back to the hub.

## Desk assignment

**Safe Surface Desk** — the caution-first, warranty-aware, manufacturer-care-guide-led desk. Stainless steel finish damage (scratching across the grain, acid etching, bleach pitting) is exactly this desk's territory. Surface Protector is the primary avatar.
