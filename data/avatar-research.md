## Seed Avatars To Validate

These are the working avatar seeds for Phase 2.

### 1. Busy floor-care upgrader

- Core job:
  "Help me keep floors cleaner without turning cleaning into another chore."
- Likely concerns:
  ease of use, battery or runtime, docking, noise, bin size, maintenance burden

### 2. Pet-mess manager

- Core job:
  "I need tools that can handle hair, tracked-in mess, and everyday cleanup without constant clogging or tangles."
- Likely concerns:
  pet hair pickup, edge cleaning, brush-roll maintenance, replacement part burden, reliability

### 3. Small-home or apartment cleaner

- Core job:
  "I need something that fits a tighter space and a lower-noise routine."
- Likely concerns:
  storage footprint, noise, charging setup, maneuverability, simpler upkeep

### 4. Deep-clean and maintenance tool buyer

- Core job:
  "When I buy a specialized tool, I want it to solve a stubborn cleaning job without wasting money."
- Likely concerns:
  cleaning payoff, real use frequency, consumables, setup complexity, long-term usefulness
