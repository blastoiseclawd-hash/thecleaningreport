## Source 1 — How to Clean Vinyl Flooring

### Step-by-step process (quoted)

**Sweep regularly:** "The best strategy for maintaining vinyl floors is to use a flat dust mop, dry mop, or other flat mop as often as you can—daily, if that's feasible."

**Vacuum before mopping:** "Always dry mop and vacuum the floor to remove surface dirt, dust, and hair before wet mopping it." Run vacuum with the rotating brush turned off, as brushes can damage vinyl.

**Mop with appropriate solutions:** "For regular cleaning, a damp mop and warm water can help keep vinyl flooring looking its best. Always wring out the mop or flat mop pad to avoid drenching the vinyl with water."

**Deep clean:** Use "a cleaner labeled as safe for vinyl floors" every few months. "These products should be pH neutral without abrasive cleaners, ammonia, or bleach."

**Address spills immediately:** "Use a soft cloth dipped in warm water to wipe up spills as soon as they occur."

### Stain removal methods (quoted)

- Baking soda paste applied with soft cloth
- Liquid dish soap solution followed by plain water rinse
- Rubbing alcohol for lipstick, dye, and ink
- Mineral spirits for crayon, paint, and marker
- Acetone-based nail polish remover for stubborn stains

### Practices to avoid (forbidden list)

Forbidden methods on vinyl include: **steam cleaning, wax polishes, bleach, ammonia, abrasive cleaners, steel wool, and wet string mops on planks.**

## Source 2 — Best Mop for Vinyl Floors (lab round)

### Testing claim (quoted)

"Bob Vila tested eight mops and ranked them by category."

### Picks (quoted)

**Best Overall:** Rubbermaid Reveal Microfiber Spray Mop — features "3 machine-washable microfiber mop pads and refillable spray bottle" with a "non-scratch rubber scrubber."

**Best Value:** Mexerris Microfiber Spray Wet Mop — lightweight at 1.6 pounds with a "360-degree rotating head."

**Best Flat Mop:** Microfiber Wholesale 18-Inch system — "18-inch mophead" for broader coverage.

Other picks mentioned: O-Cedar spray and spin mops, Yocada sponge mop, Bissell electric spin mop, Bissell CrossWave wet-dry vacuum.

### Material guidance (quoted)

Recommended types: spray, flat, spin, and sponge mops.
"Microfiber is popular because it's durable, affordable, and easy to clean."
Best option "remove[s] dust and dirt without scratching or scuffing."

### Steam-mop warning (quoted — the anchor quote for the page)

**"It is best to err on the side of caution and avoid using steam mops on vinyl floors. The high heat and moisture can cause damage if seams or adhesives are breached."**

Also: avoid "hot steam and abrasive brushes and cleaners" that could damage protective coatings.
