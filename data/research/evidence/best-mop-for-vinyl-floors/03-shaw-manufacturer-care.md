## Shaw's approved cleaner (quoted)

"Shaw R2X Hard Surfaces Flooring Cleaner" is the factory-approved cleaner for Shaw resilient vinyl flooring.

Process: "Simply spray the cleaner on the floor and use a dry mop."

For spills: "Use Shaw R2X Hard Surfaces Flooring Cleaner and wipe clean with a dry white cloth. No rinsing necessary!"

---

## Shaw's forbidden practices (quoted)

- **Harsh chemicals** — category forbidden
- **Steam** — forbidden (no steam mops, no steam cleaners)
- **Vacuums with beater bars or brushes** — forbidden (scratches the wear layer)

---

## Shaw's maintenance cadence (quoted)

- "Sweep your floors regularly to remove loose dirt and grit."
- "Wipe up any spills as soon as possible."

No explicit wet-clean frequency given; the spray-and-dry-mop routine is the manufacturer's default for general cleaning.
