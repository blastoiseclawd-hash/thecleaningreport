> **Why we don't currently recommend vacuums from iRobot**

## Verbatim extract

> iRobot's Roomba robot vacuums were once synonymous with the robot-vacuum category, but Roomba models have not performed well in our testing in recent years, and the company filed for bankruptcy in December 2025. We've evaluated a number of iRobot's vacuums and vacuum-mop hybrids, but each presented its own set of challenges and caveats:

### Roomba 694 (discontinued)

> Roomba 694 (now discontinued): Previously one of our budget picks, the 694 model was outperformed by our previous budget pick, the Eufy RoboVac 11S Max. The Roomba 694 collected only between a third to a half of the debris that the 11S Max managed to pick up, and its dustbin is considerably smaller than that of the 11S Max.

### Roomba i4/i4+ EVO (discontinued)

> Roomba i4/i4+ EVO: These models were former top picks in this guide. Though iRobot has discontinued the i4 EVO and i4+ EVO units, you may still be able to find them at some retailers.

### Roomba j9+

> Roomba j9+: The j9+ vacuum cleaned well, but like the i4+ EVO unit, its self-emptying dock didn't consistently remove all the debris from the bot's bin. Our contact at iRobot attributed this to the Cheerios in our tests, but we noticed similar issues with other, smaller debris, such as rice. This model also frequently bumped into furniture and pushed over a large cup, one of the obstacles we had put in its path.

### Roomba Combo i5+

> Roomba Combo i5+: The Combo i5+ vacuum comes with two separate, exchangeable bins — one for mopping and one for vacuuming.
>
> A major issue occurred when the app didn't automatically offer a mopping mode, which requires the scanning of a separate, easy-to-miss QR code, leading the dock to inadvertently suck dirty water into its vacuum bag. We reached out to customer service, which couldn't solve and therefore escalated the issue. But resolution was slow and came only after we contacted iRobot's PR team. (iRobot says it has since resolved the issue, and no separate QR code is needed anymore.)

### Roomba Plus 105 Vac Robot + AutoEmpty Dock

> Roomba Plus 105 Vac Robot + AutoEmpty Dock: The Plus 105 Vac Robot + AutoEmpty Dock is Roomba's entry-level model in its extensive new line. For its price (typically somewhere in between our top and runner-up picks) it's a thoroughly average performer, excelling in open spaces and struggling around corners and edges. It also has a clunky app, and its navigation is clumsy.

## Customer-service assessment

> In our research, we found that complaints about iRobot's customer service are rampant, reflecting a broader issue with its app and product support.
