## Per-product evidence summary (Surface Protector decision-tree fit)

### #1 Weiman Stainless Steel Cleaner & Polish (B00JLHNQ90) — DEFAULT PICK

- **Why it wins as default:** 3-of-3 tier2-editorial triangulation (Reviewed "Best Overall", Bob Vila "Best Fingerprint-Resistant", This Old House "Best Wipes" for the wipes variant — same formula). pH-neutral per manufacturer listing copy. Mineral-oil layer provides week-long fingerprint resistance.
- **Dealbreaker for:** Polished mirror-finish stainless (Reviewed notes "requires buffing with the grain"; mineral-oil film can show on polished finish if over-applied). Non-stainless PVD black stainless (talc scent + mineral oil interact unpredictably with PVD coating — check finish first).
- **Signature Safe Surface verdict:** "Before you buy this on a black-stainless fridge or a highly polished mirror-finish sink, test on an inconspicuous area. On brushed stainless appliance exteriors, this is the safer default."
- **Source-type labels:** editorial-triangulated (Reviewed / Bob Vila / This Old House) + manufacturer-primary (Weiman listing claim)

### #2 Therapy Stainless Steel Cleaner Kit (B078J4H5FX) — NON-TOXIC PICK

- **Why it's the non-toxic pick:** 3-of-3 editorial triangulation (Bob Vila "Best Overall", Reviewed "Best Plant-Based", This Old House "Best Plant-Based Cleaner"). 100% USDA Biobased per manufacturer listing claim. Coconut-oil-based. No petroleum solvents.
- **Dealbreaker for:** Baked-on cooked residue (Bob Vila explicitly says "not suited for cleaning baked-on or cooked-on debris"). Leaky-nozzle complaints in tier2 reviews and owner comments.
- **Signature Safe Surface verdict:** "If you have young kids or pets who are on the kitchen floor, the USDA-biobased label + no petroleum solvents is the clearest safety signal in this category. Don't expect it to cut baked-on grease — that's the pot-and-pan job."
- **Source-type labels:** editorial-triangulated + manufacturer-primary (USDA-biobased claim) + manufacturer-accepted safety certification (USDA biobased program)

### #3 Bar Keepers Friend Superior Cookware Cleanser (B000V6YLNA) — COOKWARE RESTRICTED

- **Why it's the cookware pick:** 2-of-3 editorial triangulation (Reviewed "Best for Pots and Pans", Bob Vila "Best for Cookware"). Abrasive oxalic-acid-based powder; over-100-year category lineage.
- **Dealbreaker for:** Brushed appliance exteriors (Reviewed: "can scratch coated appliance surfaces"). Any appliance with a black-stainless PVD coating. Mirror-polished finishes.
- **Signature Safe Surface verdict:** "Powder cleansers are a different tool — they solve the baked-on pot-and-pan problem no spray can touch. The same abrasiveness that removes rust from a pot will scratch a brushed fridge door. Keep it in the cookware drawer, not the appliance-cleaning caddy."
- **Source-type labels:** editorial-triangulated + manufacturer-primary (BKF abrasive-powder listing copy) + explicit tier2 incompatibility warning

### #4 Hope's Perfect Sink Cleaner and Polish (B001B47034) — SINK SPECIALIST

- **Why it's the sink pick:** 2-of-3 editorial triangulation (Bob Vila "Best For Sinks", This Old House "Best For Sinks"). Water-repellent-barrier claim on listing. 18,766 Amazon reviews at 4.3★ — strong owner base.
- **Dealbreaker for:** Mirror-finish stainless (Bob Vila explicit: "unsuitable for mirror finishes"). Appliance exteriors — it's a dedicated sink cleaner with a cream that would leave film on a vertical appliance door.
- **Signature Safe Surface verdict:** "If your main stainless pain-point is hard-water spots or the orange-brown rust halo around the drain, this is the purpose-built answer. It's not a multi-surface product — don't spread it onto your range hood or fridge expecting the same result."
- **Source-type labels:** editorial-triangulated + manufacturer-primary (Hope's compatibility list: cast iron / Corian / composite / acrylic) + explicit tier2 incompatibility warning

### #5 Magic Stainless Steel Cleaner Aerosol (B00827UD9G) — BUDGET MULTI-SURFACE

- **Why it's the budget pick:** 1-of-3 editorial (Bob Vila "Honorable Mention") + 8,546-review owner-sentiment base + Bogleheads / Houzz forum mentions. Aerosol format covers multi-appliance tile (fridge / dishwasher / oven / grill).
- **Dealbreaker for:** Polished mirror-finish stainless (Bob Vila: "slightly darkens surfaces; works best on darker metals"). Light-finish stainless sinks where darkening would be visible.
- **Signature Safe Surface verdict:** "The darkening Bob Vila flagged is real — on a darker-toned brushed fridge it actually reads as deeper shine, but on a light-toned polished sink it reads as dulling. Know your finish tone before you reach for an aerosol."
- **Source-type labels:** editorial (Bob Vila) + manufacturer-primary (Magic 17 oz aerosol appliance framing) + owner-sentiment (Amazon 8,546 + forum threads)

## Failure modes at 6mo+ (owner-sentiment layer)

From Amazon 3-star Verified Purchase reviews + Bogleheads / Houzz forum threads + cross-outlet editorial "customer notes" sections:

- **Weiman (B00JLHNQ90):** Most common 3-star complaint = "leaves streaks if not buffed in direction of grain" + "mineral oil feels greasy on skin during application." Solved by grain-aligned wipe technique + gloves for skin-sensitive users.
- **Therapy (B078J4H5FX):** Most common 3-star complaint = "nozzle leaked in shipping" + "not strong enough for baked-on stove residue." Nozzle issue is shipping-pack specific; strength issue is category-correct (plant-based formulas don't cut baked-on residue — BKF Cleanser solves that).
- **Bar Keepers Friend Cleanser (B000V6YLNA):** Most common 3-star complaint = "scratched my fancy pan" (user applied to coated / non-stick / anodized — not uncoated stainless). Surface-compatibility mismatch, not product failure.
- **Hope's Perfect Sink (B001B47034):** Most common 3-star complaint = "used too much; now the whole sink has white residue" + "only works on sinks — doesn't do anything for fridge fingerprints." Application-amount pain + reader-misfit on multi-use expectation (product is sink-specific).
- **Magic (B00827UD9G):** Most common 3-star complaint = "darkens light stainless" (matches Bob Vila flag exactly) + "aerosol spray pattern too broad for small-area targeting." Dark-surface-only framing solves the first; pump-spray alternatives (Weiman #1, Therapy #2) solve the second.
