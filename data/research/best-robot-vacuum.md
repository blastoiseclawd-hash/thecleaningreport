## Category Landscape

The broad robot-vacuum market is no longer split into only "cheap toy" and "ultra-premium flagship." The current field breaks into five practical buyer lanes:

1. **Premium all-around defaults.** These are the machines most readers should start with because they combine dock automation, decent obstacle handling, and believable mixed-floor usefulness without going all the way to flagship prices.
2. **Flagship splurge robots.** These exist for buyers who care deeply about low-clearance reach, premium navigation, and the most complete dock story. They are real, but they are easy to overspend on.
3. **Budget self-empty robots.** This lane matters because a decent auto-empty dock changes the ownership story more than many flashy software features do.
4. **Vacuum-first value robots.** Some buyers want robot convenience without paying for full mop-dock complexity. These machines can still make sense if the expectations stay honest.
5. **Dock-ownership specialists.** Bagless docks, lower consumable burden, or easier station cleanup can be a real differentiator when a household hates recurring bag costs or heavy dock maintenance.

That means the flagship page should not rank products by spec theatrics alone. It should answer which machine removes the most recurring work at each buyer tier.

## Key Insights for Writing

1. **Lead with labor removed, not app features.** The page should open around time and chore reduction.
2. **Dock upkeep counts as performance.** A machine that empties itself but creates new station cleanup is not truly lower friction.
3. **Budget robots need honest expectation-setting.** Readers should know exactly what refinement they are giving up to save money.
4. **Premium needs a reason.** Low-clearance reach, better obstacle handling, and more reliable automation are real reasons. Brand prestige is not.
5. **Bagless vs bagged dock is worth mentioning.** That is a real ownership difference, not filler.
6. **Pet hair matters even on the broad page.** It should show up in the ranking logic, but the pure pet-hair page should still carry the heavier tangle-and-upholstery story later.
