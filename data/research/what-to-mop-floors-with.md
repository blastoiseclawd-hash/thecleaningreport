## Do-not-use list (ANCHOR-READY)

### For sealed + engineered hardwood
- **Wet mops + steam mops** — NWFA primary source direct quote (captured in [evidence/02](evidence/what-to-mop-floors-with/02-nwfa-primary-source-2026-04-21.json)) per [woodfloors.org/maintenance/](https://woodfloors.org/maintenance/).
- **Vinegar / acidic cleaners** — strip polyurethane finish over time. Anchor: NWFA "cleaner specifically formulated for finish" + general pH-neutral guidance.
- **Murphy Oil Soap on modern engineered / polyurethane finishes** — leaves residue on aluminum-oxide finishes and can interfere with future refinish adhesion. Anchor: NWFA finish-specific-cleaner guidance.
- **Waxes / polish on polyurethane finishes** — creates adhesion problems for future refinish. Anchor: NWFA finish-specific-cleaner guidance.

### For natural stone
- **Acidic cleaners (vinegar, lemon, citrus)** — etch calcium-based stones (marble, travertine, limestone). Anchor: StoneTech manufacturer product-page chemistry (pH-neutral positioning) at [Amazon B00065W994](https://www.amazon.com/StoneTech-Cleaner-cleaner-24-Ounce-Bottle/dp/B00065W994).
- **Bleach on colored grout** — discolors grout (cross-referenced from `/best-grout-cleaner` research doc Clorox Plus Tilex caveat).

### For laminate
- **Excess water** — swells seams. Anchor: Bona Stone/Tile/Laminate product positioning (low-moisture RTU formulation).
- **Steam on pre-2018 laminate** — voids warranty for many brands (cross-referenced from `/best-steam-mop` research doc decision-tree warranty section).
